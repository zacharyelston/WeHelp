How it works

Patient data lives only in the practice

WeHelp app

Device key in the iPhone’s Secure Enclave. No password.

Relay

Run by WeHelp. Routes by office ID and sees only encrypted traffic.

Back Office

In the practice. All patient data, the audit chain and the staff web.

Registry

pinned TLS

outbound SSH

Which practices exist. No patient data.

The office opens one outgoing connection, so there are no open ports at the practice. The phone’s encryption ends in the practice, not at WeHelp.

Video visits

Encrypted end to end, through WeHelp’s own relay

Media is encrypted between the two devices (DTLS-SRTP). The relay forwards packets it cannot decode.

Works on any network, relay-only, so no one learns a patient’s home IP address.

Proven iPhone to iPad: HD (1280×720) on one call, two calls at once on an iPad.

Engineering test, November 2026. The visit screen in the app is in progress.

Care teams

Continuity of care, with the patient’s permission

A patient chooses which providers share one thread with them. A colleague joins only when the patient approves.

New members see earlier messages only if the patient allows it. Removing someone takes the history too.

Switches

Providers choose when they are reachable

Office hours: messages arrive any time; outside hours the patient gets an automatic reply and the provider isn’t notified.

Off: patients are pointed to the practice inbox instead.

The practice turns off what it doesn’t use, from the store to video, and the server enforces it.

17

Security

Built to pass a security audit

No patient passwords

A key in the phone’s Secure Enclave, unlocked with Face ID. Nothing to phish or reuse.

Staff on the office network

Password plus authenticator code, sessions that expire, and staff pages unreachable from the internet.

WeHelp can’t read it

The Relay passes encrypted traffic it cannot decode. Notifications carry no names.

Every change on record

An append-only, hash-chained audit trail, exportable to the practice’s compliance tools.

The bar we build to

HIPAA, OWASP ASVS Level 2 and MASVS Level 2, with an outside audit and pen test before launch.

Boring by design

Go, Postgres and SwiftUI. Every change is reviewed and tested in CI before it ships.
