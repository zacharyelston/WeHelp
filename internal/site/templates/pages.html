Get the app and join

New and current {{.Words.Patients}} use the free WeHelp app for appointments, messages, video visits and supply orders.

  1. Download WeHelp

    WeHelp is free on the App Store for iPhone.

    {{if .AppURL}}

  2. Visit the office

    Bring your phone to your next visit{{if .Phone}}, or call {{.Phone}} to arrange one{{end}}. Our staff add you as a {{.Words.Patient}}.

  3. Scan your pairing code

    Open WeHelp and tap Pair with your practice. Scan the QR code our staff show you. It works once and only for ten minutes.
